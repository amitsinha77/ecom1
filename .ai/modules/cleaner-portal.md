# Cleaner Portal

## Flow

Cleaner Login

→ Dashboard
→ Today's Jobs
→ Job Details
→ Navigation
→ Clock In
→ Perform Job
→ Job Notes
→ Upload Photos
→ Customer Signature
→ Damage Report
→ Clock Out
→ Job Completed
→ Performance

## Features

* Cleaner Dashboard
* Today's Jobs
* Job Navigation
* Clock In
* Clock Out
* Job Notes
* Photo Uploads
* Customer Signature
* Damage Reports
* Expenses
* Mileage
* Performance
* Ratings
* Payslips
* Availability
* Holiday Requests
* Training Videos

## Rules

* Cleaners can access only jobs assigned to them or their authorized team.
* Cleaner authorization must be enforced server-side.
* Cleaner location access must be limited to required functionality.
* Clock-in and clock-out events must be validated server-side.
* Job completion must be recorded with appropriate timestamps.
* Cleaner photos must use secure storage.
* Private files must not be publicly accessible.
* Customer signatures must be securely stored.
* Damage reports must be associated with the correct booking.
* Cleaners must not modify booking prices.
* Cleaners must not access customer payment details.
* Mileage and expenses must be validated.
* Payslip information must be protected.
* Cleaner availability must be validated before assignment.
* All sensitive cleaner actions should be auditable.

## Use this context

* Only when developing or modifying Cleaner Portal functionality, assigned jobs, job execution, photos, signatures, damage reports, expenses, mileage, availability, performance, or payslips.
